How to Play Pig Game

  • 🎲 Roll Dice: Roll to accumulate points into your Round Pot. You can keep rolling as many times as you dare!
  • 💥 Rolled a 1? BUST! Rolling a 1 wipes out your entire round pot and immediately passes the turn to the next player.
  • 📥 Hold: Bank your round points into your permanent Total Score and pass turn safely.
  • 🏆 Win the Game: The first player to reach the Target Score (default 50) wins!
